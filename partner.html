Who this is for

You serve them too.

If your work meets the woman who carries what was never hers, there's a way to build together.

The practitioner

You hold space for these women

Coaching, therapy, retreats, reiki, somatics, healing work. You want language and frameworks that make what you already do land deeper, and a movement to plug your work into.

The leader

You're building a culture that doesn't extract

You run a team or a company and you refuse to rebuild the thing that emptied you out. You want this in how your people work, not just in your inbox.

The collaborator

You have a room of your own

An audience, a venue, a program, a platform that overlaps with mine. You want to make something together that serves both rooms, not just cross-promote.

The shared standard

We're not building an audience. We're building the room that holds them.

Partnering here means carrying one standard into your own work: help people put down what was never theirs to hold. Not performance. Not more carrying dressed up as excellence.

The North Star under all of it is the next generation: my kids and the kids of the world, and education as the lever on what's possible for them. The 1% change compounds. And this movement lives inside the FRDTLAB ecosystem, the systems, research, community, and giving-back body that makes the work real.

You might already be holding part of the room.

Ways to build together

Four doors in.

01

License & teach the frameworks

Bring The Difference Between Love and Carrying, The Human Cost of Competence, Cultivating vs. Controlling, and Permission into your practice or program, with the language and tools to teach them well.

02

Co-create

Workshops, retreats, fireside conversations, a session inside your community. We build something for your people together.

03

The collaborator network

Refer and be referred. Cross-pollinate audiences. Show up in each other's work so the people who need this find it faster.

04

Contribute to the movement

Guest on Amplify, write for the field, or partner on a giving-back and education initiative through FRDTLAB.
