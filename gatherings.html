The room

Mission-minded women, one table at a time.

A collective committed to uplifting each other and our communities — contributing our "1%" to a wave of support and abundance. For women who want to give back, find some peace, build real friendships, and leave the world a little better than they found it.

Spread the love. One percent at a time.

What we do

Creative, journaling & art workshops.

Intentional gatherings that make space to slow down, create, and connect — rotating through the things that fill you back up.
