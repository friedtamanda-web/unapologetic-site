The Mission

Move the money to the good people.

Good people help good people, and good things happen. This is how value moves back to the ones who actually build.

Why this exists

Capital is leaking to the wrong hands.

Too much of it flows to the people who photocopied an industry they never built. The mission is simple. Reroute it. Move the money, and the capability, back toward the builders, the family-owned, the mid-market, the ethical operators. The good people. Not as charity. As repair.

How it works

Fill the cup. Pour the overflow.

You can't pour from an empty cup, so first we fill it. Real work, real income, real momentum for good people doing good things. Then the overflow pours out, into the next builder, the next family, the next kid, the next cause. And the overflow funds the next fill.

It loops. Good people help good people, and those people help the next ones. A massive shift made of small ones. One percent at a time. One leader. One business. One ripple.

My part in it

I'm one node in this.

I'm rebuilding my life after divorce, and I bet the whole thing on this. A big, joyful, slightly terrifying leap of faith. I'm raising boys, running a loud and full house, and doing the work I believe moves money into good hands. Not from fear. From watch me.

So when I say the deal is flexible, I mean it with my whole chest. You bring something good, I bring the work, and we both walk away with fuller cups than we started with. That is the mission in a single handshake. And it doesn't stop with me. When your cup is full, you fill someone else's.

How value moves

It takes more than money.

Money is one way to move value. There are so many more, and it flows both directions. Here is what fills a cup, half joking, fully open.

Money & equity

  • Equity, revenue share, project work
  • Cash, because the bills are real and there are savings funds counting on me

Trades that make the work possible

  • Software and SaaS swaps, if your tool fills my cup
  • The stuff I never want to touch again: gutters, the lawn, pool care, landscaping
  • New decking, concrete, a pool gate. Build it and I will make you look unreal on camera.
  • Laundry and household help, because a full house of boys generates a heroic amount of it

Things that keep me whole

  • A yoga membership, personal training, anything that fills the cup back up
  • A stay at your Airbnb, a boat if you're feeling generous, a little adventure

The joyfully ridiculous

  • A Jeep CJ7. A very pink Corvette. Fun clothes, always. DoorDash gift cards, unironically.
  • Or just back the cause. Donate, and let me go to work for you.

Think Cher from Clueless with a revenue model.

The teacher who refused to write anyone off. We build wealth in good hands, we have an absurd amount of fun doing it, and then we pass it on. Bring me something good. Then bring it to the next person.
