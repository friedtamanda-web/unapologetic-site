Friday
Conversations

Twelve Fridays. One room. Put down what isn't yours.

A 12-week group mentorship for women leaders who became the load-bearing wall, and are ready to lead without carrying all of it.

Competence has gravity. The more you handle, the more lands on you, until the single point of failure on your team, in your company and at home is a person. You.

Most of the women I work with don't need another course. They need a room of peers who get it, a method that works, and enough Fridays to actually change the pattern.

That's what this is.

A small group, live with me every Friday, built on the frameworks behind The Unapologetic Leader.

Asked & answered

Before you apply.

What if I miss a Friday?

The teaching portion is recorded and shared with the cohort the same day. Send your question ahead and we'll take it in the room.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Hot seats aren't recorded, and every cohort starts with an agreement: what's said in the room stays in the room.

How do I join?

Apply below. I read every application and reply within two business days. If it's a fit, you'll get the investment, payment options and a link to hold your seat.

Why isn't the investment listed?

Because fit matters more than a number on a page. Every seat is a real conversation first. You'll have the full investment and payment options before you commit to anything.

Can I still book a private session?

Yes. Note it in your application. A single 60-minute Friday Conversation counts toward the cohort, and Inner Circle includes two.

Is this advice or therapy?

It's experienced perspective and a practical method, not legal, financial, medical, or therapeutic advice.
