How to use it

Sixty seconds. One move.

01

Pull one card before the day pulls you. Morning, between meetings, or the minute before you say yes to something.

02

Read the line out loud. Each card is built on one sentence that is short enough to remember at 3pm.

03

Make the move it names. The guidebook gives every card a next move and one question to ask yourself.

Questions

Before you order.

Who made this?

Amanda Friedt, founder of FRDTLAB and The Unapologetic Leader. The lines come from twenty years of leading teams and from her own journals. It is the first release from Lipstick & Receipts.

When does it ship?

The founding edition goes to print after the final physical proof is approved. Everyone on the reserve list gets the ship date the moment it is set, before anyone else hears it.

Is this a tarot deck?

No. There is nothing to predict. It is a decision tool that looks good on a desk. Every card ends in a move you make today.

Can I order for a team or an event?

Yes. Email hello@frdtlab.com with how many decks you need and when.
