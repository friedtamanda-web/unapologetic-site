Why this exists

Competence is not consent.

You’ve learned a few things the hard way. You know which patterns you don’t want to repeat. You know that even good boundaries need maintaining.

The Practice gives you a place to return to those lessons, explore new ideas and make decisions that reflect the life you’re choosing.

Because becoming isn’t something you finish.

It’s built on the frameworks I write and teach here: Carrying vs. Caring, The Human Cost of Competence and Whose Is This?

Volume 001 · The first month

Whose Is This?

Caring is a relationship. Carrying is a transfer of ownership. Month one takes one responsibility that keeps landing on you and decides who it actually belongs to.

01 · Notice

Name the work.

Write down the task and the invisible work around it: remembering, reminding, checking and stepping in.

02 · Decide

Give it an owner.

Decide what you’ll keep, share, hand back or stop doing. Write the words you’ll actually use.

03 · Try it

Choose what comes next.

Pick one real moment to try the handoff, and decide what gets the time and attention you get back.

You keep: an ownership receipt for one real situation, the exact words to say, and a plan for the time it gives back.

What members get

What’s in a month.

The weekly letter

One short letter every week. A frame, a story from the field, and one thing to try. Five minutes to read.

One framework a month

A new original framework, taught in full. It starts with Whose Is This? and builds from there.

The workbook

A guided, printable workbook for each month’s framework, so the idea turns into a decision on your actual life.

Scripts, checklists and templates for handing things back. It grows every month, and all of it is yours while you’re a member.

How it works

No streaks. No homework.

Nothing here is a course you can fall behind in. There’s no schedule, no completion badge and no catch-up email.

Read the letter when it lands, or save it for Sunday. Open the workbook when the month gives you a gap. Skip a month and nothing breaks.

The Practice should take weight off. It should never become one more thing you carry.

Who it’s for

  • You’ve built things you’re proud of and still love learning.
  • You want thoughtful perspective, practical tools and room to explore what’s next.
  • You care about your growth without wanting another obligation.
  • You prefer something you can return to on your own schedule.

Questions

Before you join.

When does The Practice open?

Soon. The waitlist hears first, before the doors open to anyone else.

Does joining the waitlist cost anything?

No. It’s free and commits you to nothing. You’re only charged if you choose to join when it opens.

How does the founding price work?

The first 50 members pay $19 a month, and that price stays the same for as long as you stay subscribed. Billing and cancellation details come with the launch email, before you pay anything.

What if I fall behind?

You can’t. There’s nothing to keep up with. Every letter, framework and tool stays in the library, ready when you are.

How is this different from Friday Conversations?

The Practice is self-paced. It includes original resources and tools, not live calls or a member discussion forum. Friday Conversations is the separate, facilitated group mentorship experience.

What will you send me?

The waitlist notice when The Practice opens, plus The Dispatch, my free letter. You can unsubscribe from either at any time.
