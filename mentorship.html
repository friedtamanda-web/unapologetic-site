What the series will build on

Put it down. Build what’s yours.

01 · Ownership

See what you carry.

Make the hidden work visible and decide which part is actually yours.

02 · Boundaries

Practice the handoff.

Find words that sound like you, and give someone else enough room to own the work.

03 · Cultivation

Give your own work a turn.

Choose where the reclaimed time goes: your team, an idea of your own, or something you just enjoy doing.
