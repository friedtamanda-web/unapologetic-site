Lead with room to grow.

The mentorship work is now a 12-week group mentorship, Friday Conversations. A small group, live with me every Friday, for women who want to share responsibility clearly and give more of their best energy to the work and people they want to grow.

Three phases, twelve weeks

Put it down. Build what’s yours.

Weeks 1 to 4 · See it

See what you carry.

Make the hidden work visible and decide which part is actually yours.

Weeks 5 to 8 · Hand it off

Practice the handoff.

Find words that sound like you, and give someone else enough room to own the work.

Weeks 9 to 12 · Build what’s yours

Give your own work a turn.

Choose where the reclaimed time goes: your team, an idea of your own, or something you just enjoy doing.
