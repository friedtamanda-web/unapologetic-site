We're not waiting for the system to change.

One Step Forward puts real resources into Detroit K–5 classrooms, books, basics, and burnout relief, so every child can learn and every teacher can lead. One kid, one coat, one classroom at a time.

Why this exists

The debates happen far from the room where a teacher is buying her own books.

Detroit teachers are leaving the profession at roughly double the national rate. In many K–5 classrooms, chronic absenteeism runs above 65%. K–3 reading scores fell 20–30% through the pandemic. We work in that room, the actual classroom, not the conversation about it.

One kid, one coat, one classroom at a time.

Figures drawn from DPSCD and public Detroit-education research.

What we do

Four ways we show up.

01

Teacher support

Classroom grants, basic needs, and burnout relief for the people holding it together.

02

Literacy & readiness

Books and culturally relevant materials, plus reading volunteers for the K–3 readers hit hardest by learning loss.

03

Classroom basics

The unglamorous infrastructure: a washer and dryer so kids have clean clothes, resource-room build-outs, storage, warm rooms kids want to show up to.

04

Measure what matters

Attendance, literacy progress, and teacher satisfaction, tracked and reported honestly.
