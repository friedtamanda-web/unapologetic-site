The ownership receipt · A workshop example

Whose is this?

What I keep catching
I finish the team update and remind everyone to send their part.
What’s mine to own
I’ll assemble the update. Each lead owns getting their numbers in by Thursday.
What I’ll say
“Send your numbers by Thursday at noon. If they’re late, I’ll mark them pending instead of chasing.”
What I want that time back for
An hour for the work I keep pushing to Friday.

One real situation

What we’ll do together.

01 · Notice

Name the work.

Write down the task and the invisible work around it: remembering, reminding, checking, and stepping in.

02 · Decide

Give it an owner.

Decide what you’ll keep, share, hand back, or stop doing. Write the words you’ll actually use.

03 · Try it

Choose what comes next.

Pick one real moment to try the handoff. Decide what gets the time or attention you get back.

Led by Amanda Friedt
