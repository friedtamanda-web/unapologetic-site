Privacy Policy

Effective June 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FRDTLAB and Amanda Friedt ("we," "us," "our") handle information in connection with theunapologeticleader.com and frdtlab.com (the "Sites"). We aim to collect as little as possible and to be clear about the rest.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you subscribe to the newsletter, book a conversation, buy a product, or email us, you may share your name, email address, payment details, and anything you choose to tell us. Payment details are handled by our payment processor and are not stored by us.

Information collected automatically. The Sites are static and don't run our own tracking. Our host and the embedded third-party tools below may collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, as described in their own policies.

2. How We Use It

  • To send the newsletter and respond to you.
  • To schedule, deliver, and follow up on conversations and sessions.
  • To process payments and fulfill product orders.
  • To understand, maintain, and improve the Sites.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third parties, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Substack — newsletter signups and delivery.
  • Calendly — scheduling conversations.
  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Gumroad — digital product sales and delivery.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.
  • Google Fonts — typography.

When you interact with these tools (for example, booking through Calendly or paying through Stripe), your information is handled under their terms, not ours.

4. Cookies

Our pages don't set our own marketing or tracking cookies. Embedded third-party tools (such as Calendly, Stripe, or Substack) may set their own cookies when you use them. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers above as needed to run the Sites and Services, or when required by law.

6. Your Choices & Rights

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@frdtlab.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, and we will honor valid requests.

7. Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete or anonymize it.

8. Children

The Sites are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version, with its effective date, will always be posted here.

10. Contact

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Email hello@frdtlab.com.
